About

Hi, I’m Tatevik. 👋

I’m a freelance Growth & AI Product Designer, most recently 5 years at Picsart. I design AI-powered consumer products where user behaviour, experimentation and product clarity meet.

I start with evidence: funnels, recordings and user research come before any design. I test ideas with A/B tests and guardrail metrics, so a win is a real win. I also use AI, ChatGPT and Claude for research, Claude and Figma Make for prototypes, to move faster without cutting corners.
